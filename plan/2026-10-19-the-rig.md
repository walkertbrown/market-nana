# Plan 2026-10-19 — monetize the story: sell the rig, tested before it's built

Move: RULES §2.6, **sell the story**, turned into something the story's own
crowd can buy. Product: the studio. It differs from round 1 (pitching writers
and podcasts): round 1 gets the story told. This round sells to the people who
come to read it.

Why: the boss, 10-19 (FACTS): "I think that it proves we have to figure out how
to monetize the story. Not the apps". The evidence is in TRIED and LEARNED. One
r/ClaudeCode comment by the boss was seen 15,637 times and brought about 400
visits, roughly 1 in 40 viewers, to the home page and the zoo. None of them
bought anything, and the site has no email signup, so none of them can be
reached again.

## What market-gate said (10-19)

Verdict: **proceed with changes**, for one thing only.

- **The one live option: the operating kit, "the rig".** That's the mailroom,
  the adversary and market-gate agents, the key guard, the rules-file pattern
  and the zoo-site generator: everything that let six bots run unattended in
  public for 40 days. The buyer is a Claude Code Max subscriber who wants
  several bots running without leaking keys. Sell it as "6 bots, 40 days, 4,204
  commits, in public". Never sell it as "make money".
- **Comparable kits:** AgentsKit ($49–89, claims 35 builders) and ClaudeKit
  ($14.99/month or $99 lifetime, just launched). Neither shows checked
  traction. Free subagent collections set the floor.
- **Killed:** a paid ebook or course (guides sell a result, and $18 isn't one),
  sponsorship (too little traffic), consulting (it spends the boss's hour), a
  paid "check my slop" (a commodity; keep it free, it collects this crowd's
  emails) and a paid book or talks.
- **Kept:** a free weekly email, and unpaid podcasts and press (round 1
  already does those).
- **New, unproven:** a hosted public log, "your bots' zoo", as a monthly
  service. Tested as a second button only.
- **Price range for the boss:** kit $49–99, one-time. Avoid $19–29, where
  Puzzle Press already sits.
- **Against it:**
  - The rules files are already public in the bots' repos.
  - Anthropic now ships scheduled Routines.
  - Buyers' install problems would eat the boss's hour.
  - "Claude" is Anthropic's mark, and ClaudeKit, AgentKit and AgentsKit are
    taken.
  - One good comment is about 400 visits. Even at 2% buying, that's side
    income, not a business.

## The cheapest real test

Nothing is built until people pay for it.

1. **A page, built by docent** (§2.4: I build no pages): /kit on the studio
   site.
   - It lists what's in the rig and shows the boss's price.
   - It has three buttons: a refundable pre-order ("refunded in full if it
     hasn't shipped by <date>"), "tell me about the hosted log", and "just the
     weekly email".
   - It also adds an email signup to the home page and the zoo, so the next
     crowd isn't lost like the last one.
   - The page is at its own path, so the browser beacon counts its visits.
2. **One comment by the boss, as himself**, in a thread already about agents
   running businesses, with real news. The news: "My sixth bot is a marketing
   bot. The first thing it found: one Reddit comment of mine brought more
   visitors than 40 days of my bots' marketing put together." I draft it in
   outbox/ with the Market-Nana line. He rewrites it in his own voice. The kit
   is mentioned only when someone asks how the bots are set up.
3. **The boss's time:** about 40 minutes. Pick the price, post one comment,
   reply over two nights.

No spend. No new account, if the studio's existing payment account can make a
pay link. If it can't, that goes to the boss.

## The number that says it worked

**Leading:** pre-orders and emails from the /kit page.

- **Pass:** at least 3 paid pre-orders, or at least 40 emails.
- **Fail:** 0 pre-orders and under 15 emails. The kit dies, and the weekly
  email keeps whatever list there is.
- **Void:** the comment reaches under 2,000 views. Retry once, in another
  thread.

**Scoreboard:** a paid pre-order is a paid customer of the studio. Refunds
count against it.
