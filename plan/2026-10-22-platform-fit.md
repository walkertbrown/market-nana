**The adversary (10-22):**
- **Per-pair counted links can't work.**
  - Shorts descriptions have no clickable links since 2023, and Instagram captions have none.
  - A TikTok account has one bio link.
- **The numbers are too small.** At about 100 views a clip, each product-platform pair gets 0 to 2 visits in 14 days, which is noise.
- **TikTok's flat views say nothing about topic fit.**
- **X for VibeCider is a guess,** not a result.
- **Extra accounts start from zero,** and identical reposts can be cut on Instagram.
- **The data points to search, not feeds:** the Google sale and the YouTube KDP how-to.

**So the test changes:**
- **The number** is what each platform already reports per clip, with no links needed:
  - average watch time or percentage watched;
  - like rate;
  - shares and follows.
- **Grouped by audience:**
  - Shopify owners: Sizecurve, Close of Day, Flatdesk;
  - KDP self-publishers: Puzzle Press, Trace Press, KDP how-tos;
  - teachers and parents: the worksheets;
  - indie builders: VibeCider, the agent story;
  - hobby: round robin, comic covers, Bulkhead.
- **Where it comes from:** the boss's exports from all three platforms (YouTube and TikTok are in; Instagram is needed), now and on 11-05.
- **Judging, 11-05 (14 more days of clips):**
  - an audience earns a platform if its clips there beat the platform's own average on watch time and like rate;
  - an audience that loses on all three gets no clips;
  - an audience that wins only on YouTube goes to YouTube search how-tos.
- **New accounts:** only for an audience that wins on TikTok and is drowned out on the shared account.

**Not measured here:** Shopify App Store search, where the Shopify apps are most likely found. "Listing tweaks" is closed in TRIED unless the boss strikes it.
