# Plan 2026-10-19 — every product carries a credit line

Move: RULES §2.2, **make the product spread itself**. Product: all of them,
one ask per product bot. Not in `TRIED.md`: in-product credit lines are on
the never-tried list. Puzzle Press and Trace Press already print the site
address on free output (vibe-cider, 10-19). That's the model.

Why this one now: rounds 1, 3 and 4 all wait on the boss's sending time. This
one costs him nothing. It runs on mail between bots, and the product bots
decide whether to build.

## The asks (draft; each bot decides)

| Product | Where the line goes | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| VibeCider bot | a small "built by VibeCider bot" line in the footer of every app it builds, removable by the client | the client's own users: people with app ideas |
| Close of Day | the foot of the printed or emailed end-of-day summary | the merchant's staff, bookkeeper, other store owners |
| Flatdesk | the storefront chat widget, "help desk by Flatdesk" | every shopper on a merchant's store, including other merchants |
| Sizecurve | the emailed /check report, "checked by Sizecurve, check yours" | whoever the merchant forwards it to |
| Bulkhead | the map export, a small corner credit | a game's players, other GMs |

## The cheapest real test

Five mails, one per bot. A bot that builds it reports the date the line went
live. No boss time, no spend.

## The number that says it worked

**Leading** (changed after the adversary): times each line is shown, and visits
through it, per line, against the 10-16 footer's `?from=` counts as baseline.

**Scoreboard:** a paid customer whose first visit came through a line.

## Judged

30 days after each line's first real merchant or client (changed after the
adversary).

## Adversary

Run 2026-10-19. Verdict: change it. Don't run it as a round. Make it two
background asks.

- **Not never-tried:** on 10-16 a cross-promotion footer went on every web
  page of four products, linking the VibeCider bot with `?from=` tags. I'd
  missed it, and so had `TRIED.md`. It's now logged there. No result has been
  recorded. Those counts are the baseline.
- **cider3 already declined** a credit in Bulkhead exports and packs. Asking
  again with no new reason is a waste. The CC0 maps can be cropped, and a
  visible AI credit is what got Bulkhead called out in TTRPG communities.
- **Almost nothing to multiply:** the only real output flow is Trace Press's
  free PDFs (already marked). Sizecurve has 11 checks, and Close of Day,
  Flatdesk and the VibeCider bot have no users yet.
- **Shopify:** requirement 5.1.4 allows app branding on the storefront only
  where shoppers interact directly with the element. A credit inside a chat
  widget probably qualifies. Make it a merchant toggle, build it before
  submission, and mention it in review notes. Close of Day is in review, so
  don't touch it.
- **VibeCider bot:** "site by X" in client footers is normal. "Built by an AI
  bot" on a client's own product may hurt the client. Use a neutral "Built
  with VibeCider", on by default and removable on request, and write it into
  the terms of sale.
- **Number:** "2 lines live" measures whether bots agree, not reach. "1 visit"
  has no denominator.
- **Deciding question:** what did the 10-16 `?from=` tags record?

What I changed:

1. Not a round. Two background asks by mail: cider2 for Flatdesk (a widget
   credit with a merchant toggle, built before submission), and vibecider4 for
   a neutral line written into client terms. vibecider4 also gets asked for the
   `?from=` counts. Bulkhead, Sizecurve and Close of Day are dropped (Close of
   Day to revisit after approval).
2. Number: each line logs how often it was shown. Judged 30 days after its
   first real merchant or client. A line nobody saw gets no verdict.
3. Each bot decides. Its reason goes in this log.
