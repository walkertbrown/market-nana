# Plan 2026-10-19 — Puzzle Press through KDP educators, for Christmas

Move: RULES §2.1, **borrow someone else's buyers**, with §2.5 (do the work out
loud) as the thing we hand them. Product: Puzzle Press, with Trace Press as the
second link. Not in `TRIED.md`: no bot has written to a creator, educator,
course seller or newsletter that holds KDP sellers. (Two list writers were
emailed on 10-17 about their tool lists. That's §2.4, a different ask, and it's
still open; this play doesn't write to them again.)

## Why now

- Puzzle Press's one sale came from a KDP seller who searched for a tool and
  paid 26 minutes later. The buyer exists; they don't know we do.
- KDP sellers learn tools from a few YouTube channels, newsletters and course
  sellers. Those people need fresh, seasonal "how to make a Christmas puzzle
  book" material every October and November.
- Christmas deadline: KDP's own timelines page says low-content reviews can take
  3–4 weeks, there's no pre-order for low-content books, and each format is
  capped at 2 new titles a week. A seller who wants Christmas books live has to
  upload in early-to-late November, so has to hear about the tool in the next
  two weeks. That gives the educator a timely video and the seller a reason to
  act.

## Who it reaches

About eight people who teach KDP puzzle or activity books to an audience:
YouTube channels with puzzle- or low-content tutorials posted in 2026, a
low-content newsletter, and one or two course or kit sellers. Some sell their
own tools or kits, which makes them less likely to promote ours; the list
favours teachers over vendors. Names live in `outbox/` only.

## What they get

1. **A worked example they can show:** a finished Christmas word-search book
   (interior and full-wrap cover, KDP-ready) made in Puzzle Press, with the
   minutes it took and every setting. Market-Nana builds it in the free tool
   and describes it; the boss checks it before anything is sent.
2. **The full version free for them,** if vibe-cider agrees, so they can show
   it without the PREVIEW mark.
3. **Something for their audience:** depends on what vibe-cider will do (a
   tracked link at minimum) and, for a cut, on the boss (money: §5).

## The cheapest real test

Eight short emails from the boss. Each names the educator's own recent video
or post, attaches or links the worked example, and offers the free unlock. It
says Market-Nana drafted it.

## The number that says it worked

**Leading:** an educator shows or links Puzzle Press (video, newsletter,
resource list) by 2026-11-09, which is still in time for a Christmas upload.
Worked = at least 1 of 8. Replies are counted too.

**Scoreboard:** a paid Puzzle Press or Trace Press unlock whose source is that
mention (tracked link, or the buyer says so).

## Judged

2026-11-09 for mentions; 2026-11-30 for sales.

## Adversary

Run 2026-10-19. Verdict: change it; don't run it as written.

- **Dead end:** there's no money in it for educators. Book Bolt pays affiliates
  25% recurring and runs its own tutorial channel. A 25% cut on a one-time $19
  is about $4.75 once, and mentioning us could cost them Book Bolt commission.
  Several educators sell their own generators or kits. The site says "Built by
  an AI bot", and the KDP forum thread calling the bot spam is one search away.
- **My deadline was wrong:** KDP's help page says puzzle books are generally
  not low-content, so the 3–4 week low-content review probably doesn't apply.
  The Christmas urgency rested on it.
- **Offer:** a free unlock is worth nothing to them. What might work: "$19 once
  against a Book Bolt subscription" as a content angle, a done-for-them
  story-led Christmas book per educator, a per-audience code, a 50% cut.
- **The question that decides it:** do small KDP channels make "cheap tool vs
  subscription" videos at all?

## What I found, and the decision

I checked that question (two searches, 10-19). No such videos turned up.
What did turn up: at least five **free, no-watermark** KDP puzzle generators
(PuzzleForge, Univers Studio, kdpbook.io, KDPage, PuzzlePage), several claiming
KDP-ready PDFs. A comparison video would set Puzzle Press, with $19 to remove a
PREVIEW mark, against those, and Puzzle Press loses.

**Killed before any work or spend.** Nothing was sent. The finding went to
vibe-cider by mail, because it's about the product's position more than its
marketing. What Puzzle Press offers that the free tools don't is vibe-cider's
call. If it names something real (the full-wrap cover with the spine worked
out, say), the educator play can come back with that as the angle.
