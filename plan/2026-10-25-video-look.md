# Round 8: the videos all look the same

Opened 10-25 at 22:20 CDT, from the boss.

## Why
- The boss sent a grid of 8 clips, each at 90 to 116 views: "They all look like the same video". After my caption mock-ups:
  "I wish the videos looked like that. Idk if yall are gonna be good enough at making videos. Figure it out for me" (FACTS).
- One template on every clip (white screen, dark box, all caps, same spot) and four audiences on one feed. Every clip stops
  at the app's first test audience; video links have brought 3 arrivals since 10-21.

## What I found (22:35)
- A bot can make the look with no camera. Remotion (React to MP4, renders in its own headless browser) built an 8-second
  1080x1920 proof on this machine in under two minutes: the Thanksgiving sheet on a wood table, a crayon filling "pumpkin",
  a lowercase outlined caption at the top, a "20 words · free to print" pill at the end. Sent to the boss.
- The proof is a wipe, not real strokes. Trace Press already has every letter's stroke paths and start dots, so the
  real version can draw the crayon along the actual strokes, in stroke order. That's the satisfying part.
- Licence: Remotion is free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people (its licence page; check before use).
- Honesty: my first mock caption spoke as a teacher ("for 20 kids"). That invents a person; changed to the studio's own voice.

## The proposal (mine to make; the product bots build, the bots that post, post)
- One product, one audience first: Trace Press's seasonal sheets, for parents and teachers. Thanksgiving is about 4 weeks out.
- Six caption looks (outbox/caption-styles.html), never the same one twice in a row.
- Cheapest test: 3 clips in the new look, real stroke order, posted the usual way.
- Number: median views of the 3 at least 3 times the old ~100, and at least 1 arrival on /tt, within 7 days of posting.
- Judged at the first weekly TikTok read after they post (10-29 if they're up by then; else 11-05).

## Adversary
- ~~The ~100 floor may be the account~~: the boss, 22:55, already split the accounts by audience; the grid was from before,
  and the new accounts' clips still look the same. So the look is the lever left.
- The YouTube export (09-23 to 10-21, 39 Shorts): median watch 3.7 seconds, 12% of the clip. The tracing clips are the
  worst (1.2 to 1.6 seconds). The best (KDP gutter margins, 120 views) held 48 seconds on a 26-second clip: it looped.
  People leave on the first frame. That is the problem the new look must fix, and the number to judge it by.
- Animated paper can read as an ad. 20 seconds of real footage (a printed sheet, a hand, a crayon), filmed once by the
  boss on his phone, could sit under many clips. Optional.
- Who builds it: vibe-cider owns Trace Press and its clips; it isn't mine to build. The sheet carries it to the boss.
