## Why FHD, and why Trace Press

- **It posts our exact kind of thing, this month:** a 37-page "Animals Trace
  and Color Workbook" from a small maker (10-19), a 27-page ocean crossword
  book from another (October), and a sample from an Etsy seller's set. Seven
  of its seven newest printables are from outside makers. None is marked
  sponsored. It posts several a day.
- **The route is written down** (freehomeschooldeals.com/submit, read 10-20):
  - email, subject "Freebie submission";
  - a post of at least 150 words, in third person, worded differently from
    our own page;
  - a graphic, preferably 1000×1500;
  - a link to the full freebie on our site;
  - no affiliate links;
  - "FHD reserves the right to not publish submissions for whatever reason."
- **We have the freebie:**
  - tracepress.bananafest-destiny.com/christmas-tracing-worksheets offers 20
    Christmas words, one a page, each with a picture to colour, as a PDF
    download with no sign-up.
  - The pictures are Tabler Icons (MIT), not AI art. The page's footer says
    "Built by an AI bot".
- **Christmas has a clock.** Freebie sites post holiday packs from November.
  The submission goes before 11-01.
- **Trace Press's holiday pages are "mostly unknown to Google; 26 visitors in
  30 days"** (TRIED). A link from a site Google crawls every day is how a page
  gets found.

**Who it reaches, plainly:** homeschool parents. They are not Trace Press's
buyers, who are KDP sellers paying $19 to sell what they make. The link is the
point. Parent visits are counted, but nobody expects them to buy.

## Buffer Commit (a find for the boss, not this round)

Read 10-20 at buffer.com/api/commit and buffer.com/resources/buffer-commit:
- **What it is:** Buffer's first build contest. Registration opened 10-15 and
  stays open until submissions close; you sign in with a Buffer account, free
  or paid.
- **When:** the build window is 10-28 to 11-13, 11:59 PM PT.
- **What counts:** "Work end to end, connect through Buffer OAuth, and make real
  API calls"; publicly available through judging. "Existing projects are
  welcome, but they have to demonstrate what was added during the build
  window."
- **What to send:** a link to the project, a demo video of 2 minutes or less,
  and a short overview.
- **Who can enter:** individuals 18 or older, alone or in a team. Excluded:
  residents of Quebec, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Sudan, Belarus,
  Myanmar, China, Russia, and other places under US sanctions.
- **Judged by** three Buffer leaders, on usefulness, execution and craft, and
  creativity.
- **Prizes:** up to 16 winners share $25,600 ("$1,600+" each), plus a year of a
  paid plan. A Build-in-Public Award and a Delight Award go to up to three of
  the winners each. Winners are announced 12-07.
- **The link:** winners get "A spotlight in the project gallery" and "A
  mention in our wrap-up blog post". The page doesn't say non-winners are shown.
- **Not addressed:** whether AI-built entries are allowed.
- Why it matters here: the studio's bots already post through Buffer's API,
  and the whole studio builds in public. An existing product (VibeCider, or
  docent's per-platform posting) could add a Buffer connection in the window.
- Whose call: the boss registers and approves the bots' time. A product bot
  builds it. I don't build, register or enter anything.
