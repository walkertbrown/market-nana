# Round 15: ShelfCall, a note to bar trade editors (10-26)

**Why:** The boss, about 21:25: "Shelfcall is live as web app and android app.
Time for you to use to do your job and get it seen". Round 14 (teachers) is
about links. This round is about bar managers reading about it.

**What the search ruled out (a market-gate pass, 21:28 to 21:33):**
- **Bar-inventory creators:** the biggest bar-inventory YouTuber runs a competing
  app. The rest of the 2025-26 "how to do bar inventory" pages are by vendors.
  Nobody independent is left to write to.
- **Bartending schools:** the students don't buy inventory software. The online
  bar-management certificates are UK sites, and ShelfCall's buyer is a US bar.
- **The trade press is what's left.** One on-premise trade magazine has a named
  on-premise editor, with an address shown on its contact page. A restaurant
  trade magazine has run launch notes for two bar inventory apps (2018, no
  byline, probably press releases). A third bar magazine runs "featured
  products", but its site blocks a fetch, so it's held until the boss sees an
  address in a browser.

**The move:** the boss emails 2 trade editors (3 if the third address shows) a
short note: a bar inventory you count out loud, free for one area, on the web
and Android. Each note offers the free tenths chart and calculator to readers,
and invites the editor to count their own home shelf in two minutes.
- **Who it reaches:** US bar and restaurant managers who read the trade press.
- **Cheapest test:** 2 or 3 emails from the boss, sent by 10-28.
- **Number:** pass if an editor replies (asks for a login, a demo or more detail)
  or the magazine mentions ShelfCall. Zero of both closes trade-press notes for
  ShelfCall until there's a customer quote or a measured count time.
- **Judging date:** 11-16.

**The adversary:**
- With no user quote, a new free app is thin news.
- Trade editors favour advertisers.
- The line saying a bot drafted it may read as mass PR.
- Even a printed note brings a handful of visits, not a stream.

The cure for the first is one real count at a real bar, with a quote and a
before-and-after time. I asked the boss whether his bar has done one. If it
has, the note leads with it.
