## Adversary

Run 2026-10-19. Verdict: change it.

- **Dead end:** a vendor's comparison that ranks itself is the commonest thing
  in this niche: at least five KDP-tool vendors publish "best puzzle maker"
  posts with themselves on top. Ours adds an AI author, a forum record of
  "spam", and a cold email.
- **The table has a hole:** it leaves out Book Bolt (which has a puzzle
  generator) and Puzzle Maker Pro (a long-standing desktop tool sold as a
  one-time licence). So "$19 once instead of monthly" isn't unique. The honest
  line is "$19 once, in the browser, the whole book with cover." Calling it
  "six tools tested" without the two incumbents looks cherry-picked.
- **Credible only if checkable:** a dated screenshot per cell, no ranking,
  the incumbents tested or marked untested, and rivals' paid tiers marked
  untested.
- **Targets:** independent writers who reach puzzle sellers barely exist. Three
  could be verified: two KDP Substacks (one wrote about the new 2-a-week limit
  on 10-16; one argues sellers don't need expensive tools) and an
  activity-book template seller whose kit complements us. Send 3, not 5.
- **Better carrier:** Puzzle Press's own site, because the only sale came from
  Google. Publish the table as a dated page, linked from the spine calculator.
  Too slow for Christmas. Also a guess: offer the "PuzzleForge for the
  interior, Puzzle Press for the cover" pairing to free tool makers.
- **Peg:** KDP's 2-paperbacks-a-week limit (from 09-21, third-party reports).
  At 2 books a week, a monthly plan costs a seller far more per book than $19
  once. That gives a writer a reason to run the numbers.
- **Number:** 1 of 5 by 11-13 is useless for Christmas (11-19 submit-by) and
  too few to learn from. Early kill: no reply by 10-31. Mention by 11-07. A
  paid unlock by 11-30.
- **Deciding question:** will any independent writer who reaches puzzle
  sellers ask the boss for the screenshots by 10-31?
